Drop your WAV files here. Each filename maps to a game event:

  eat-good.wav        — Eating healthy food (fruit, salad, etc.)
  eat-bad.wav         — Eating unhealthy food (burger, fries, etc.)
  ghost-hit.wav       — Hit by a stress ghost
  email-hit.wav       — Hit by an email enemy
  blob-hit.wav        — Hit by a blob enemy
  footstep.wav        — Normal footstep
  footstep-boost.wav  — Fast/boosted footstep
  stress-warning.wav  — Stress man nearby warning
  tired-puff.wav      — Wheezy sound when health is low
  level-complete.wav  — Level finished
  heart-attack.wav    — Game over / heart attack
  burnout.wav         — Burnout state sound
  relax.wav           — Relaxing / stress going down

Files not present = game falls back to the built-in synthesizer sound.
WAV format recommended. Files are loaded and cached on first use.
